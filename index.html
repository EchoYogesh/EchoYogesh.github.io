The wedding day · Sunday 31 January

The baraat and the day

The order of the day, in the order it happens. Times for the rituals are set by the family priest on the day.

  1. Baraat arrives

    The groom's party reaches the bride's home with the band and the horses. The bride's family welcomes them at the door.

  2. Milni

    The two families meet, one pair at a time, with garlands and embraces — the moment the two households become one.

  3. Jaimala

    Brijesh and Sangeeta exchange garlands. The one moment everyone is waiting to photograph.

  4. Pheras

    The couple takes the seven rounds around the sacred fire, with the priest guiding each vow.

  5. Sindoor & mangalsutra

    Brijesh applies sindoor and ties the mangalsutra — the marriage is complete.

  6. Dinner

    A long, unhurried meal. Please stay and eat with us.

  7. Vidaai

    The bride leaves with her husband, throwing rice back over her shoulder — a blessing for the home she grew up in.

The baraat muhurat is 4:00 pm on Sunday 31 January 2027. Every other time on this day is set by the family priest.

Getting there

Travel and stay

By train

The nearest station is Pratapgarh Junction. From there it's about 35 km to the village — roughly an hour by car or auto.

By road

Pratapgarh sits on NH-330. From Lucknow it's roughly three and a half hours, from Allahabad about two. The village is 4 km off the main road.

By air

Lucknow (Chaudhary Charan Singh) is the closest airport, about 150 km away. Allahabad (Prayagraj) is the next closest, around 90 km.

Where you'll sleep

Tents will be pitched in the garden of the family home for guests who are staying over — simple, clean and close to everything. Let us know in the RSVP and we'll save you a tent.

Questions

A few things you might ask

Is there a dress code?

No strict code. Marigold, gold and deep reds always look right; anything you'd wear to a family wedding in UP is perfect. Please avoid black and white.

Can I bring children?

Of course. There will be space for them to run about, and a quiet room if anyone needs a nap. Just mention numbers in the RSVP so we can plan food.

Where should I stay?

Tents will be pitched in the garden of the family home for guests staying over, so there's no need for a hotel. If you'd rather have a hotel, Pratapgarh has a few; ask us and we'll point you to one.

Is it an arranged marriage?

Yes. There's no meet-cute to tell — just two families who found each other, and a couple who are very happy to be chosen for this.

Will there be photographs?

A photographer will be there for the Shaadi and the Reception. The Haldi and Mehndi will be captured more casually — feel free to take your own.

What if I can only come for one function?

Come for whichever you can. Every function is a complete event on its own, and we'd rather see you for an hour than not at all.
