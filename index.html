Five occasions, at the family home

Mehndi

How the day unfolds

The baraat arrives at the muhurat of 4:00 pm (4 o'clock). After that the rituals set the pace, not the clock.

  1. Baraat

    4:00 pm (4 o'clock), at the gate: dhol, dancing and rather more people than planned.

  2. Milni

    Both families meet at the entrance with garlands and embraces. This is where the noise begins.

  3. Jaimala

    Brijesh and Sangeeta exchange garlands, and the pandit begins the ceremony.

  4. Kanyadaan & Pheras

    The seven sacred vows around the holy fire. Please gather close to bless the couple as they begin their married life.

  5. Sindoor & Mangalsutra

    Blessings, a few tears, and the moment the two of them are formally married.

  6. Dinner

    A festive traditional vegetarian feast featuring hot puris, rich regional curries, live chaat, and royal sweets.

  7. Vidaai

    Sangeeta embarks on her new journey with the Yadav family. Please join us at the gate for heartfelt blessings and warm farewells.

The ceremony will be conducted in Hindi and Sanskrit. We'll keep it easy to follow, and there will be someone at every row to explain what is happening.

Agni Sakshi · Vedic Traditions

The Seven Sacred Vows

Around the sacred Agni, Brijesh and Sangeeta will take seven steps together, binding their lives in love, devotion, and lifelong companionship.

01 Pratham Phera

ॐ एकमिषे विष्णुस्त्वान्वेतु

Nourishment & Sustenance

A shared vow to nourish each other with health, pure food, kindness, and devotion throughout life's journey.

02 Dwitiya Phera

ॐ द्वे ऊर्जे विष्णुस्त्वान्वेतु

Strength & Protection

To stand as pillars of emotional, physical, and spiritual courage for each other and shield our home in every season.

03 Tritiya Phera

ॐ त्रीणि रायस्पोषाय विष्णुस्त्वान्वेतु

Prosperity & Righteous Wealth

To earn our livelihood through noble and righteous means, sharing blessings with charity, humility, and grace.

04 Chaturtha Phera

ॐ चत्वारि मायोभवाय विष्णुस्त्वान्वेतु

Family Harmony & Joy

To cherish both our families, honor our elders' wisdom, and cultivate harmony, laughter, and unity in our household.

05 Panchama Phera

ॐ पञ्च पशुभ्यो विष्णुस्त्वान्वेतु

Compassion & Progeny

To pray for healthy, virtuous future generations, treating every living soul with empathy, gentleness, and respect.

06 Shashtha Phera

ॐ षड् ऋतुभ्यो विष्णुस्त्वान्वेतु

Health Across All Seasons

To pray for wellness through all the seasons of nature and all the changing chapters of life, side by side.

07 Saptama Phera · The Eternal Bond

ॐ सखे सप्तपदा भव सा मामनुव्रता भव

Sacred Friendship & Oneness

Having taken seven sacred steps together, we become lifelong companions in love and duty; may our holy bond remain eternal.

Travel & stay

Reaching Chandauka

The village sits in Pratapgarh district, between Lucknow and Prayagraj. Trains are the easiest way in; from the station, we'll help with the last hour.

By train

Pratapgarh Junction is the closest station, with direct trains from Lucknow, Prayagraj, Delhi and Varanasi. Tell us your train and we'll arrange a car from the station.

By air

Prayagraj and Lucknow are the nearest airports, both a few hours by road. If you let us know your flight schedule in advance, we will gladly arrange local transport from the airport.

By road

The village is off the Chandauka road in Kohandaur. From Lucknow or Prayagraj, take the highway towards Pratapgarh and turn at Chandauka; ask for the Yadav house and anyone will point you on.

Where you'll sleep

Warm village hospitality awaits you. Traditional guest accommodation with comfortable bedding, warm quilts, and continuous hot chai is arranged at the family home.

Questions

Things people have asked

What should I wear?

Traditional Indian festive attire: vibrant sarees, lehengas, kurtas, sherwanis, or formal suits. The rituals involve floor seating, so comfortable festive wear is recommended.

Anything particular for the haldi?

Shades of yellow, mustard, and marigold are encouraged. Traditional cottons and comfortable clothes suitable for playful turmeric celebrations are ideal.

And for the mehndi?

Festive green and mint shades are lovely for the evening. Breezy or short sleeves are recommended so you can relax while intricate henna is applied.

How far is everything from each other?

All celebrations are hosted at our family home in Chandauka. The courtyard, veranda, garden, and mandap are all within a two-minute walk of each other.

Where do I sleep?

All accommodations are arranged directly at our family home with cosy garden tents, warm quilts, and continuous hot chai. Please mention the number of guests in your RSVP so we can keep everything ready.

Is there food for everyone?

The kitchen is pure vegetarian, with Jain and vegan plates on request. If you have an allergy, put it in the RSVP and it will be kept aside for you.

Can I bring my children?

Of course; weddings here are family affairs. Tell us how many of you are coming so the places and the small plates can be counted.

What is the weather like in January?

Pleasant winter sunshine by day and crisp, chilly village evenings. A warm pashmina shawl, blazer, or Nehru jacket is highly recommended.
